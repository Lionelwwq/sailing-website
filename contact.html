FAQ

Good questions, quick answers.

Can't find what you're looking for? Call 437-477-8897.

Can you supply products to my exact specifications?

Yes. Glass is cut and processed to your requirements, and every accessory is supplied according to customer requirements. Send us sizes, quantities, loads, finishes or a drawing.

How do I get a price?

Add items to your quote list as you browse, then send the form above, or call 437-477-8897. Quotes are free and cover everything on your list in one reply.

Are the specifications on the website exact?

They are typical values to help you compare products. Final specifications, options and lead times are confirmed in your quote.

I'm not sure which product fits my process. Can you help?

Yes. Book a free consultation and tell us about your line, the glass you work with and the problem you're solving. We'll recommend the right product.

Do you install as well as supply?

Yes. We install windshields, storefronts, windows and doors, and take on custom bath and shower projects, with a warranty on products and installation.

Who do you work with?

Residential, commercial and automotive customers: homeowners, builders, fabricators, installers and businesses.

Custom glass, glass cutting and replacement, plus the materials and accessories the glass industry runs on.
