Our story

More than three decades in glass.

Sailing International Trade has supplied the glass trade since 1993. Today we supply all types of glass for residential, commercial and automotive work, install windshields, storefronts, windows and doors, and source the accessories and equipment that fabricators and installers rely on.

We work with partners, global customers and end users with a focus on pragmatism, innovation, openness and cooperation, so every project gets the right product at a competitive price.
